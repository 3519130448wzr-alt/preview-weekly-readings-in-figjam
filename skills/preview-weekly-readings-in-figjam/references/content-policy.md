# Content policy

## Source boundary

- Treat the supplied article PDF, assignment sheet, syllabus, and course materials as the complete evidence base by default.
- Browse only when the user explicitly requests or authorizes external research. Clearly separate external context from article-derived claims.
- Read the full article before writing the synthesis. Distinguish theory, illustration, empirical evidence, author inference, and your own explanation.
- Cite PDF page numbers for section overviews, important mechanisms, comparisons, and final synthesis claims.

## Translation and copyright

- Keep the original PDF outside FigJam. The learner reads it locally while the board acts as a companion.
- Use short English anchors only—normally a heading or several words needed to locate the paragraph.
- Provide faithful Chinese interpretive translation, not a line-by-line English duplicate or an uploaded scan.
- Preserve essential English terms in parentheses when the Chinese equivalent is ambiguous or the term is central to the article.
- Do not upload PDF page images, reproduce tables or figures wholesale, or place long English passages on the board.

## Section overview

At the first unit of every new section, explain:

1. the question this section answers;
2. how it follows the previous section;
3. the mechanism or distinction to watch;
4. the concepts and examples that matter;
5. the section's resulting conclusion.

Do not repeat the translation. Explain argumentative function.

## Concepts and vocabulary

Place concepts and vocabulary immediately under the relevant translation. Each entry should include:

- English term or phrase;
- concise Chinese meaning;
- meaning in this article's context;
- first meaningful page;
- a contrast or common confusion when useful.

Prefer high-value vocabulary: academic verbs, theory-building nouns, causal language, contrast signals, evaluation terms, methods vocabulary, and repeated discipline-specific expressions. Exclude names, citation boilerplate, and words that are obvious in context.

Default density is 16–22 combined items per page-level unit, or 12 vocabulary items plus 4 concepts for a wider page-range unit. Density is a target, not permission to add noise.

## Evidence language

Use precise labels:

- `作者主张` for a stated proposition;
- `概念示例` for an illustration;
- `经验证据` only for reported data or analyses;
- `待验证推论` for propositions not tested in the article;
- `不能据此推出` for an important inference boundary.

## Course AI policy

If the syllabus contains an AI policy, summarize it accurately in the header. Otherwise use a neutral notice: the companion is for preview and comprehension, and must not be copied into assessed work as the learner's own analysis.

## Question analysis area

The area must contain only:

- the bilingual title `问题解析 Question Analysis`;
- a large editable blank canvas.

Do not add questions, prompts, labels, answer frames, tags, examples, or model answers.
